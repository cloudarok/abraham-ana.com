FAQ

At the courthouse

Which entrance do we use?

The main entrance under the Great Arch on Anacapa Street. After security, take the three steps on your right to the elevator and stairs. The Mural Room is on the second floor.

What is security like?

Monday is a court day, so expect airport-style screening at the door. Shoes stay on, but belts with metal buckles go through the X-ray. Leave pocketknives (even keychain ones) and selfie sticks at home; they can be confiscated and aren’t returned.

Where should I park?

City Lot 7 is directly across Anacapa Street. The first 75 minutes are free, then it’s $3 an hour. Street parking downtown is limited to 75 minutes and overtime is ticketed. Coming from the hotel? A rideshare skips parking entirely.

Can I take photos?

Yes, please do. Just keep your flash off inside the Mural Room; flash photography isn’t allowed there.

Can we toss petals or confetti?

Please don’t. The courthouse doesn’t allow petals (real or faux), confetti, rice, birdseed, glitter or sequins anywhere on the property.

Can I bring a coffee or water?

Food and drinks, water bottles included, aren’t allowed in the Mural Room. The whole property is also smoke-free and vape-free.

Is the Mural Room accessible?

An elevator goes to the second floor, and drivers with a disabled placard get the first 2½ hours free in city lots. Tell us about any needs in your RSVP and we’ll plan for them.
